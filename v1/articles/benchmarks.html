Benchmarks

How fast Egami is next to ImageSharp, SkiaSharp and Magick.NET on the same tasks and inputs. This page has 260 comparisons (each case against each library timed on it). Egami is faster in 234 of them, within 5% in 16, and more than 5% slower in 10, led by lossless WebP at effort 4 on screen content, where SkiaSharp's native libwebp is about 10 times faster. Against ImageSharp, Egami is typically 1.5 to 3 times faster.

Performance has the tips that keep your own code fast and the benchmarks that ship with Egami.

How it was measured

The numbers come from a comparison harness kept apart from the Egami repository, so that Egami itself never references the other libraries. Most areas were run between 9 and 22 October 2026 against Egami's main branch on that day. JPEG and PNG were rerun on 30 October against Egami cd0e6b41 (decode, after the speed work in #208) and 35d45808 (encode); WebP was rerun the same evening against 6b8b07b9. Compare libraries within one run only: absolute times differ a little between runs.

  • Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 3900X, 12 cores and 24 threads, Windows 10 22H2, .NET 10.0.12 x64 (AVX2).
  • Libraries: ImageSharp 3.1.12, SkiaSharp (native Skia, libpng and libwebp), Magick.NET Q16 (native ImageMagick).
  • Job: BenchmarkDotNet ShortRun: 3 warmups and 3 iterations. Error bars are wide on some rows, so treat differences under about 10% as a tie.
  • Threads: "1 thread" sets Egami's MaxParallelism and ImageSharp's MaxDegreeOfParallelism to 1; "All cores" leaves them at the processor count. SkiaSharp and Magick.NET choose their own threading.
  • Left out: drawing, text, SVG, barcode and video speed are timed separately and are not on this page.
