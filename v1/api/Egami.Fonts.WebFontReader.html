Unwraps WOFF 1.0 and WOFF 2.0 files into the TrueType or OpenType font they hold, as WebFontWriter wrapped it: the reverse of the writer, for tools that want the sfnt back.

Examples

// Back from WOFF2 to a TrueType font a desktop app installs: glyf, loca and hmtx rebuilt, every other table as it was.
byte[] ttf = WebFontReader.ToSfnt(woff2File);
Typeface installed = Typeface.FromMemory(ttf);

Remarks

WOFF 1.0 tables are inflated as they were stored. A WOFF2 file's glyf, loca and hmtx are rebuilt from their transformed forms (WOFF 2.0, sections 5.1 and 5.4), so glyf's glyphs read the same as the original's but may be laid out more compactly; a WOFF2 collection becomes a TrueType or OpenType collection (.ttc) with its shared tables stored once. The container's extended metadata and private data are not part of the sfnt; GetWoffMetadata() and GetWoffPrivateData() read them.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The data is not a WOFF or WOFF2 file.

CorruptDataException

A table does not decompress, or a transformed table cannot be rebuilt.

LimitExceededException

The font would be larger than the context's MaxTotalBytes.
