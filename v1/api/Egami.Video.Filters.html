Namespace Egami.Video.Filters

Classes

Deinterlacer

Turns interlaced frames into progressive ones. Each output frame keeps one field's lines and makes the other field's: Adaptive keeps the woven lines where the picture holds still and guesses along edges where it moves, using the fields before and after; Bob guesses from the kept field alone. Every plane is done on its own lines, so a 4:2:0 field keeps its own chroma rows; 8- to 16-bit samples, any chroma format and alpha work. The filter holds one frame back to look at the next.

DeinterlacerOptions

How a Deinterlacer works. The defaults deinterlace the frames the decoder flags, one frame out per frame in.

Interfaces

IVideoFilter

A streaming video filter on decoded planes: frames go in through Send(YuvVideoFrame) and come out through TryReceive(out YuvVideoFrame?), the same shape as decoders, encoders and IAudioFilter, so a filter sits between a decoder and an encoder. A filter may hold frames back (Latency) and may hand out more or fewer frames than it took, such as one per field.
