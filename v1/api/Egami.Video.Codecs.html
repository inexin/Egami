Namespace Egami.Video.Codecs

Classes

Av1Codec

AV1, as WebM, Matroska, MP4, IVF and raw OBU files carry it, decoded by Egami's AV1 decoder in the Main, High and Professional profiles: 8, 10 and 12 bit, 4:0:0, 4:2:0, 4:2:2 and 4:4:4, film grain, scaled references and scalable streams, every frame equal to libaom's sample for sample. A packet is a temporal unit. Frames come out at their own size (a stream may change size at a key frame) as 8-bit planes, or 16-bit planes holding 10 or 12 bits; a monochrome stream gives 4:0:0 planes. Hidden frames give no frame; a frame shown later with show_existing_frame comes out then. Decode only.

Av1Packets

Helpers for AV1 packets, which hold a temporal unit of low-overhead OBUs (AV1 specification section 5, each OBU with its size field): split a unit into its frames and merge them back, and build the av1C configuration record MP4 and Matroska store for a track.

CodecHeaderReader

Lists the header fields of a track's packets read in order, keeping what a codec's later headers depend on. For AV1 it decodes each temporal unit with Egami's Av1StreamDecoder, so each frame header is listed whole, as the decoder read it (LastHeaders, under the specification's syntax element and variable names), in place of the start Describe(TrackInfo, EncodedPacket) gives. Other codecs give what Describe(TrackInfo, EncodedPacket) gives.

CodecHeaders

Lists what a compressed frame's header says, field by field under the codec specification's names, for tools such as egami-video info --headers and for checking an encoder's output. VP8 (RFC 6386 sections 9 and 19), VP9 (VP9 bitstream specification sections 6.2 and 6.3) and AV1 (AV1 specification section 5) are described; later codecs add theirs to the same call. An AV1 frame header depends on the reference frames, so CodecHeaderReader reads a track's packets in order to list them whole.

MotionJpegCodec

Motion JPEG: every frame a complete JPEG image, decoded and encoded by Egami's JPEG codec. Decoding fixes frames without a DHT segment, weaves interlaced field pairs, ignores junk around the image and decodes several frames at once. Frames come out as the JPEG's own YUV planes, BT.601 full range unless the container says otherwise.

RawVideoCodec

Uncompressed video (Uncompressed): I420, YV12, Y42B, 444P, Y800, NV12, NV21, YUY2, UYVY and v210 by FourCC (FourCC), plain planar YUV at 8 to 12 bits with optional alpha as in YUV4MPEG2, and AVI's bottom-up 24-bit BI_RGB (as VfwFourCC), whose frames come out as G, B and R planes (Identity). Decoding and encoding copy samples, so they are lossless.

VideoCodec

A video codec: which tracks it decodes and how to create its decoder and encoder. Register a new one with WithCodec(VideoCodec).

VideoCodecs

Creates decoders and encoders from the codecs registered in a MediaContext.

VideoDecoderOptions

How a decoder decodes.

VideoEncoderOptions

What an encoder makes.

Vp8Codec

VP8 (RFC 6386), as WebM, Matroska and IVF carry it, decoded by Egami's Vp8StreamDecoder: every frame equals libvpx's sample for sample. Frames come out as 8-bit 4:2:0 planes, BT.601 limited range unless the container says otherwise; hidden frames (alternate references) give no frame. A WebM track with alpha (HasAlpha) decodes its second VP8 stream, carried in each packet's addition 1, into the frame's alpha plane unless DecodeAlpha is off. Decode only.

Vp9Codec

VP9, as WebM, Matroska and IVF carry it, decoded in all four profiles: 8, 10 and 12 bit, 4:2:0, 4:2:2, 4:4:4 and RGB, with every frame equal to libvpx's sample for sample. Frames come out at their own coded size (a stream may change size between keyframes) as 8-bit planes, or 16-bit planes holding 10 or 12 bits; hidden frames (alternate references) give no frame. A WebM track with alpha (HasAlpha) decodes its second VP9 stream, carried in each packet's addition 1, into the frame's alpha plane unless DecodeAlpha is off. 4:4:0 streams are not decoded. Decode only.

Structs

CodecHeaderField

One field of a compressed frame's header, as Describe(TrackInfo, EncodedPacket) lists it.

Interfaces

IVideoDecoder

Turns packets into frames with a send and receive loop. One packet can give no frame yet (a codec that holds frames back) or several, so after each Send(EncodedPacket) call TryReceive(out YuvVideoFrame?) until it returns false, and at the end of the stream call Flush() and receive what is left.

IVideoDecoder<TPixel>

A decoder that converts its frames to TPixel pixels. See IVideoDecoder.

IVideoEncoder

Turns frames into packets with a send and receive loop, the mirror of IVideoDecoder. After each Send(VideoFrame) call TryReceive(out EncodedPacket?) until it returns false; at the end call Flush() and receive what is left.
