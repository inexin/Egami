Parameters

MaxTotal float

The largest total of the printing inks on one pixel, as a fraction (3.2 is 320%).

MaxAt Point?

Where that total is (the first such pixel), or null for a raster with no ink.

Limit float

The total ink limit measured against, as a fraction.

ShareOver double

The share of pixels whose total exceeds Limit, 0 to 1.

PerPlate IReadOnlyDictionary<string, double>

Each plate's mean coverage, 0 to 1, by ink name; technical inks included.
