Namespace Egami.Metafiles

Classes

Metafile

A Windows metafile, WMF, EMF or EMF+ (and their gzip forms WMZ and EMZ), read and checked; its records stay internal (design question 3). The size facts come from the header: an EMF's frame, a placeable WMF's box and units per inch, or a plain WMF's window extent at 96 DPI. A metafile is immutable and safe to read from many threads.

MetafileDecodeOptions

How MetafileFormat draws a metafile for Raster.Load and sizes it for Raster.Probe.

MetafileFormat

WMF, EMF and EMF+ (and WMZ, EMZ) as an Egami format (design question 16): registered with WithFormat(FormatInfo), Raster.Load draws a metafile at its own size at 96 DPI (or Dpi) onto a transparent Rgba8 raster, and Raster.Probe gives that size from the header without drawing. Text uses the installed fonts. For your own fonts, a target size, a background or the GDI part of a dual file, load a Metafile and call Rasterize<TPixel>(MetafileRenderOptions?).
