Class Fade

Namespace
Egami.Video.Audio.Filters
Assembly
Egami.Video.dll

Fades the sound in or out over a stretch of the stream's time line. A fade in is silent before Start and reaches full level on the exact sample at Start plus Duration; a fade out is full level before Start and silent from its end on. Times are on the frames' time line, rounded to whole samples. The curves give the same gains as ffmpeg's afade with the curve each value names. Works in place.
