Apple's Core Audio Format (.caf): one audio track of PCM (integer or float, either byte order), G.711, ALAC, Opus, AAC, MP3, AC-3 or FLAC, with 64-bit chunk sizes, so no 4 GB limit. The packet table (pakt) cuts variable-size packets and gives the priming frames (CodecDelay) and the padding at the end; the magic cookie (kuki) becomes CodecPrivate; the info chunk becomes Tags; markers and regions, named from the strg chunk, become chapters; and a chan chunk gives the channel layout. Other formats pass through undecoded with an "X_CAF/" codec id. The writer streams PCM and G.711 (a data chunk sized -1 on a non-seekable output) and puts the packet table of other codecs after the samples, or before them through a temporary file when the output is not seekable. Seeking is sample exact for PCM and packet exact for the rest.

Examples

// CAF has no 4 GB limit and streams PCM: on a pipe the data chunk simply runs to the end of the file.
using (AudioWriter writer = AudioWriter.Create("tone.caf"))
{
    writer.Write(tone);
}

// Opus, AAC, MP3, AC-3, FLAC and ALAC packets are cut by the packet table, which also gives the priming frames.
using (AudioWriter writer = AudioWriter.Create("tone-opus.caf", new AudioWriterOptions { Codec = AudioCodecs.Opus }))
{
    writer.Write(tone);
}

AudioTrackInfo opus = (AudioTrackInfo)MediaFile.Probe("tone-opus.caf").Tracks[0];
Console.WriteLine($"{opus.CodecId}: {opus.Duration}, {opus.CodecDelay} priming");

Creates a muxer for one audio track: integer PCM (8, 16, 24 or 32 bits), float PCM (32 or 64 bits), G.711, ALAC, Opus, AAC, MP3, MP2, AC-3 or FLAC. Attachments and cover art are left out with a "metadata-dropped" warning.
