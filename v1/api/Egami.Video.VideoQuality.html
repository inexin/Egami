Class VideoQuality

Namespace
Egami.Video
Assembly
Egami.Video.dll

Measures how close decoded video is to a reference, frame by frame or over a whole clip: PSNR and SSIM per plane, and MD5 hashes of frames and packets in the layout FFmpeg's framemd5 uses (planes one after another, rows without padding, little-endian 16-bit samples above 8 bits).

VideoQuality quality = new();

    quality.Add(reference.Raster, encoded.Raster);

Psnr

Gets the PSNR in decibels over every frame added: per plane from the plane's total squared error, and the average from all planes' squared error together. Infinity when the frames are identical.

public PlaneScores Psnr { get; }

Property Value

PlaneScores

Ssim

Gets the SSIM over every frame added (1 for identical frames): per plane the mean of the frames' SSIM, which is the mean over 8 by 8 windows at a 4-pixel step; the average weights the planes by their sample counts.

public PlaneScores Ssim { get; }
