What Analyze(IReadOnlyList<Av1AnalysisFrame>) found for one frame: how much of each 16x16 block's content the frames after it predict from it. A plan carries it (Analysis) to give the blocks that matter most finer quantizers on a group's base frames, or lower rate-distortion multipliers on the others.

Av1StreamDecoder

Decodes an AV1 video stream (AV1 Bitstream and Decoding Process 1.0.0 with errata 1) a temporal unit at a time, as WebM, Matroska, MP4 and IVF files carry it: key, inter, intra-only and switch frames with every inter tool of the Main, High and Professional profiles, 8 to 12 bits and 4:0:0 to 4:4:4, film grain, scaled references and scalable streams. The frames equal libaom's and dav1d's sample for sample. Containers are not read here: Egami.Video reads them and uses this decoder, and any other source of AV1 temporal units can too.

Av1StreamEncoder

Encodes an AV1 video stream (AV1 Bitstream and Decoding Process 1.0.0 with errata 1) a frame at a time: key, intra-only and inter frames, inter blocks predicted from up to seven references with every single and compound mode, inter-intra, overlapped and warped motion, and hidden frames shown later with show_existing_frame. Each frame's Av1FramePlan says what it is (its type, whether it is shown), its quality or quantizer, and which reference slots it reads and refreshes, so rate control and group-of-frames planning stay with the caller: Egami.Video drives this encoder for its containers, and any other source of frames can too. The frames decode in libaom and dav1d exactly as in Av1StreamDecoder.
