Vp8StreamDecoder

Decodes a VP8 video stream (RFC 6386) a frame at a time, as WebM and IVF files carry it: key frames and inter frames, with the last, golden and alternate references, motion vectors and hidden frames. The frames equal libvpx's sample for sample. Feed the frames in stream order; decoding starts at a key frame. Containers are not read here: Egami.Video reads WebM and IVF and uses this decoder, and any other source of VP8 frames can too.

Vp8StreamEncoder

Encodes a VP8 video stream (RFC 6386) a frame at a time: key frames, and inter frames that search the last, golden and alternate references for motion and code every VP8 vector mode. Each frame's Vp8FramePlan says what it is (key or inter, shown or hidden), its quality or quantizer, and which references it uses and refreshes, so rate control and group-of-frames planning stay with the caller: Egami.Video drives this encoder for WebM, MKV and IVF, and any other source of frames can too. The frames decode in libvpx exactly as in Vp8StreamDecoder.

WebpWriterOptions

Options for writing WebP. The defaults follow libwebp's: quality 75 and effort (libwebp's method) 4. A raster read from a WebP file keeps its kind, lossless or lossy; anything else is written lossy.
