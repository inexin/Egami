Examples

// Black and white with every setting spelled out.
TraceResult ink = scan.Trace(new TraceOptions
{
    Mode = TraceMode.BlackAndWhite,
    Threshold = null,      // null: Otsu's method, as Raster.FindThreshold; or a level from 0 (black) to 1 (white)
    PathFidelity = 0.5f,   // 0: fewest curves; 1: curves that follow the pixels most closely
    Corners = 0.75f,       // 0: round every corner; 1: keep every corner (a polygon)
    Noise = 4,             // regions under 4 pixels merge into the regions around them
    IgnoreWhite = true,    // the ink alone, no white background shape
});

// Or start from a preset and change one thing.
TraceResult polygon = scan.Trace(TraceOptions.BlackAndWhite with { Corners = 1 });

BlackAndWhite

Gets the defaults with IgnoreWhite on: the ink alone, for signatures, line art and logos to place over other content.

TraceOptions

Colors

Gets the most colors (in Grayscale, gray levels) a grayscale, color or logo trace reduces the image to, from 2 to 256. The colors are the image's own flat ones, found with the blends along edges left out; an image with fewer distinct colors gets fewer. Defaults to 8. Not used in black and white or when Palette is set.

public int Colors { get; init; }

Corners

Gets how readily corners stay sharp, from 0 (round every corner) to 1 (keep every corner: the outline is a polygon). Defaults to 0.25, which keeps right angles sharp and, in grayscale and color, any turn of more than 60 degrees.

public float Corners { get; init; }

Default

Gets the defaults: black and white at the Otsu threshold, with the paper traced as a white background shape, curves within half a pixel, turns of more than 60 degrees kept as corners and single-pixel specks dropped.

TraceOptions

IgnoreWhite

Gets whether white areas are left out. In black and white, when false (the default), a white shape the size of the image comes first, under the ink, wherever the image has paper. In grayscale, color and logo, the layer of each palette color whose red, green and blue are all at least 240 is left out, and its areas stay empty in the other layers; in logo, that includes the transparency the art encloses, which is otherwise traced as white.

public bool IgnoreWhite { get; init; }

Noise

Gets the smallest region kept, in pixels: a region of fewer pixels merges into the regions around it, smallest first. 0 or 1 keeps every pixel. Defaults to 2, dropping single-pixel specks and holes.

Palette

Gets fixed colors for a grayscale, color or logo trace, or null (the default) to find them in the image. Each pixel takes the nearest of them (by intensity in grayscale; in logo, each of the image's own colors does), and the shapes take these colors exactly. The list is copied.

PathFidelity

Gets how closely the fitted curves follow the pixel edges, from 0 (loose: fewest curves) to 1 (tight: most curves). Defaults to 0.5: in black and white, joined curves stay within 0.2 pixels of the curves they replace; in grayscale and color, the curves stay within 0.5 pixels of the edge points.

public float PathFidelity { get; init; }

Smoothing

Gets how strongly a grayscale or color trace smooths the image before reducing its colors, from 0 (not at all, the default) to 1. The smoothing keeps edges between clearly different colors and flattens texture and noise, for photos. Not used in logo, which takes flat-color art only.

Threshold

Gets the level that splits ink from paper, from 0 (black) to 1 (white): a pixel is ink when its intensity, over white by its alpha, is at most this. null (the default) finds it with Otsu's method, as FindThreshold(ThresholdMethod, ThresholdMode) does. Used in black and white only.
