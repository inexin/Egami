Properties

Entry

Gets the entry Load returns, by its index in the file's directory, or null (the default) for the best: the largest, then the deepest (a PNG entry counts as 32 bits), then the first. With MinimumSize and no entry named, the smallest entry at least that big is chosen before any reduction.
