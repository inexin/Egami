Barcode

A barcode symbol: its format, its modules, the quiet zone its standard asks for and the human-readable text that goes with it. It draws on any canvas as vector paths (CanvasBarcodeExtensions), becomes one Path, or renders to a raster with whole-pixel modules. A barcode is immutable and safe to use from many threads.

BarcodeDrawOptions

How DrawBarcode(Canvas, Barcode, RectF, Paint, BarcodeDrawOptions?), ToPath(float, BarcodeDrawOptions?) and ToRaster(int, BarcodeDrawOptions?) draw a barcode.

BarcodeModules

The modules of a barcode symbol: a grid of dark and light squares for a 2D symbol, or a row of bars and spaces for a linear one. Every encoder ends in one, and drawing, rasterizing and path building work from it. It holds the symbol only, without its quiet zone. A grid is immutable and safe to read from many threads.

BarcodeReader

Reads barcodes from images: raster.ReadBarcodes() finds every symbol, raster.ReadBarcode() the first. The reader is thread-safe and allocates per call only.

Gs1

Reads GS1 element strings from their human-readable form, such as (01)09501101530003(17)261231(10)AB12, and checks each against the GS1 General Specifications: the AI is defined, the characters and lengths fit it, and check digits, dates and times are right. GS1-128, GS1 Data Matrix and GS1 QR encode the result.

Gs1ElementString

One GS1 element string: an Application Identifier and its data field, checked against the GS1 General Specifications (characters, lengths, check digits, dates and times). Parse(string) makes a list of them from text such as (01)09501101530003(17)261231, and the GS1 symbols encode that list.
