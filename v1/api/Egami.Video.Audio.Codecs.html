Namespace Egami.Video.Audio.Codecs

Classes

FlacCodec

FLAC (RFC 9639), lossless: decodes every stream the format allows (fixed and LPC prediction, every stereo mode, block sizes up to 65535, 4 to 32 bits per sample) into planar integer frames, checking the frame CRCs and STREAMINFO's MD5; encodes at levels 0 to 8 with the reference encoder's meaning, default 5. Read and written in .flac files, Matroska and MP4.

OpusCodec

Opus (RFC 6716 with the RFC 8251 updates), decode only: SILK, CELT and hybrid frames at every bandwidth, mono, stereo and the multichannel mapping families 0, 1 and 255 (RFC 7845), with packet loss concealment and in-band FEC. Frames come out as planar float at 48 kHz, Opus's own rate, in the canonical channel order; other rates go through a Resampler. The decoder is translated from libopus 1.5.2. Ambisonic mappings (families 2 and 3) are not decoded.

PcmCodec

Uncompressed PCM: integer samples of 8 (unsigned), 16, 24 or 32 bits, little-endian (Instance, WAV's) or big-endian (BigEndian, AIFF's and QuickTime's twos), and 32 or 64-bit float (FloatingPoint). Each instance decodes all three codec ids; it encodes its own. Decoded frames are interleaved, in the stored sample type: 24-bit samples come as S32 with ValidBits 24.

VorbisCodec

Vorbis I, decode only: every stream the specification allows (floors 0 and 1, residues 0 to 2, channel coupling, all block sizes), written from the Vorbis I specification and within 2^-22 of libvorbis on the test files. Frames come out as planar float in the canonical channel order (Vorbis puts center second); the first block's overlap and the end padding are trimmed. Read from Ogg, WebM, Matroska and MP4, where the track's codec private data holds the three header packets, Xiph-laced.
