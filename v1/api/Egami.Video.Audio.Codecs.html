Namespace Egami.Video.Audio.Codecs

Classes

FlacCodec

FLAC (RFC 9639), lossless: decodes every stream the format allows (fixed and LPC prediction, every stereo mode, block sizes up to 65535, 4 to 32 bits per sample) into planar integer frames, checking the frame CRCs and STREAMINFO's MD5; encodes at levels 0 to 8 with the reference encoder's meaning, default 5. Read and written in .flac files, Matroska and MP4.

G711Codec

G.711 (ITU-T G.711) μ-law and A-law, decode only: one byte per sample, expanded to 16-bit samples by the standard's tables. Read from WAV (format tags 7 and 6), AVI, AIFF-C (ulaw, alaw) and MP4/MOV. Each instance decodes both laws. Frames come out interleaved as S16.

Mp3Codec

MPEG audio (ISO/IEC 11172-3 and 13818-3), decode only: Layers I, II and III (MP3) of MPEG-1, MPEG-2 and MPEG-2.5, every rate from 8 to 48 kHz, mono, stereo, joint and dual channel, free-format bit rates, and the CRC-16 when frames carry it. Frames come out as planar float, one per MPEG frame (1152 samples, 576 for MPEG-2 and 2.5 Layer III, 384 for Layer I). Read from .mp3 files and from MP3 and MP2 tracks in AVI, Matroska and MP4. The decoder is translated from minimp3.

OpusCodec

Opus (RFC 6716 with the RFC 8251 updates). Decodes SILK, CELT and hybrid frames at every bandwidth, mono, stereo and the multichannel mapping families 0, 1 and 255 (RFC 7845), with packet loss concealment and in-band FEC. Frames come out as planar float at 48 kHz, Opus's own rate, in the canonical channel order; other rates go through a Resampler. Encodes 1 to 8 channels at 8, 12, 16, 24 or 48 kHz (mapping family 0 for mono and stereo, 1 above) in every mode, with the settings of OpusEncoderOptions; the default is 64 kbit/s per channel, variable bitrate, 20 ms frames. Both are translated from libopus 1.5.2 and match its output exactly. Ambisonic mappings (families 2 and 3) are not supported.

OpusEncoderOptions

How the Opus encoder codes: the bitrate and how strictly it is kept, what the audio is, the frame duration, how hard the encoder searches, the widest band and the loss protection. Every one has a default that suits music and most speech.

PcmCodec

Uncompressed PCM: integer samples of 8 (unsigned), 16, 24 or 32 bits, little-endian (Instance, WAV's) or big-endian (BigEndian, AIFF's and QuickTime's twos), and 32 or 64-bit float (FloatingPoint). Each instance decodes all three codec ids; it encodes its own. Decoded frames are interleaved, in the stored sample type: 24-bit samples come as S32 with ValidBits 24.

VorbisCodec

Vorbis I, decode only: every stream the specification allows (floors 0 and 1, residues 0 to 2, channel coupling, all block sizes), written from the Vorbis I specification and within 2^-22 of libvorbis on the test files. Frames come out as planar float in the canonical channel order (Vorbis puts center second); the first block's overlap and the end padding are trimmed. Read from Ogg, WebM, Matroska and MP4, where the track's codec private data holds the three header packets, Xiph-laced.
