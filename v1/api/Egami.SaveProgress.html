How far a save such as Save(string, WriterOptions?, IProgress<SaveProgress>?, CancellationToken) has come. A save reports once as it starts, then at most every 250 ms, then once more with IsFinal set.

Examples

// Slow encoders (AVIF, JPEG XL, lossy WebP) report how far they have come: once as they start, at most every 250 ms,
// then once more with IsFinal. A canceled token stops the encode and deletes the half-written file.
Progress<SaveProgress> progress = new(p => Console.WriteLine($"{p.Fraction:P0} after {p.Elapsed.TotalSeconds:F1} s"));
image.Save("duck.avif", options: null, progress, cancellationToken);

Fraction

Gets how much of the save is done, from 0 to 1, or null when the format's writer does not say (it then reports only as it starts and ends). Formats that encode the whole image at the end, such as AVIF, JPEG XL and lossy WebP, count their own work, not the rows handed to them. It never goes backwards, and the final report has 1.
