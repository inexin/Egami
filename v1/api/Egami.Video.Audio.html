AudioFrame

A block of decoded audio: SampleCount samples for every channel of its Layout, stored as its Format says, with the time of the first sample. The audio twin of VideoFrame: decoders hand frames out, encoders take them, and the memory comes from the context's memory source, so dispose each frame.

ChannelMixer

Mixes one channel layout into another with a matrix: each output channel is a weighted sum of the input channels. The default matrix follows ITU-R BS.775: a speaker both layouts have passes at full level; a missing center goes to front left and right at -3 dB (0.7071); missing surrounds go to the nearest speakers left (side to back and back to side, at full level when that only renames the pair and at -3 dB when it folds a second pair in, else to the front at -3 dB); a mono output takes left and right at -3 dB and surrounds at -6 dB; LFE is dropped unless asked for. Upmixing only routes the speakers both layouts share, so no channel is made up. Channels with no position map by index.

LoudnessMeter

Measures loudness as ITU-R BS.1770-5 and EBU R 128 define it: momentary (400 ms) and short-term (3 s) loudness while audio comes in, and at any point the gated integrated loudness, the loudness range (EBU Tech 3342), the true peak and the sample peak. It takes any sample format, any rate from 8 to 384 kHz and any channel layout, weighting surround channels by +1.5 dB and leaving the LFE channel out. The audio filters' loudness normalization measures with it too.

Resampler

Converts audio between any two sample rates with a polyphase windowed-sinc filter (Kaiser window). The filter's phases come from the rate ratio reduced to lowest terms; when that needs more than 1024 phases (as for unusual rates), coefficients between 1024 precomputed phases are interpolated. It works in 32-bit float and keeps time exact: the first output sample has the first input sample's timestamp and every later one follows at the output rate, so a resampled track stays in sync. Frames of any length and format go in through Send(AudioFrame); frames come out through TryReceive(out AudioFrame?), in OutputFormat and OutputLayout, so conversion and channel mixing run in the same pass.

Structs

ChannelLayout

Which speaker each channel feeds: a set of Speakers whose channels come in the order of their bits (WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE order, the one canonical order every decoder hands out), plus any channels with no position, such as ambisonic or unknown ones, after them. Decoders of codecs with another order (Vorbis and Opus put center second) remap their channels to this one.

LoudnessValue

One loudness reading in a LoudnessMeter series.

Interfaces

IAudioDecoder

Turns packets into audio frames with the same send and receive loop as IVideoDecoder: after each Send(EncodedPacket) call TryReceive(out AudioFrame?) until it returns false; at the end call Flush() and receive what is left.

IAudioEncoder

Turns audio frames into packets, the mirror of IAudioDecoder. Frames may have any length and any sample format; the encoder converts them to its own and cuts packets of FrameSize samples.

Enums

ResamplerQuality

How hard a Resampler works: the length and steepness of its filter.

SampleFormat

How an AudioFrame stores its samples: the type of one sample, and whether the channels are interleaved (one buffer, a sample of every channel in turn) or planar (one buffer per channel). Integer samples are signed except U8, whose silence is 128; float samples run from -1 to 1.

Speakers

Speaker positions, with the bit values of WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE's channel mask. Channels in a ChannelLayout come in the order of these bits, lowest first, which is the order WAV and FLAC store them in.
