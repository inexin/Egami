A raster read as coverage, 0 (none) to full, placed on the image being edited: where an operation inside Within(Mask, Action<RasterOps>) applies and how strongly. The raster may have any size and pixel type; outside it coverage is 0, or full when Inverted. Its first frame is used for every frame. It must stay undisposed until the chain has run.
