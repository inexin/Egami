Namespace Egami.Formats.Extended.Fits

Classes

FitsCard

One header card: a keyword, its value and its comment. The value is a string, a bool, a long, a double, or null for a card without one (COMMENT, HISTORY, a blank keyword). A value the reader does not type, such as a complex number, is kept as its text.

FitsDetails

Details only FITS has, kept in FormatDetails: the HDU's header and how its values became the picture. The writer writes the header's own keywords (all but the structural ones) back.

FitsFile

A whole FITS file, for the numbers rather than a picture: every HDU's header cards, and an image's physical values, BZERO + BSCALE × stored, unstretched, with missing pixels (BLANK, NaN, damaged tiles, data past the file's end) as NaN. Tile-compressed images decode as plain ones. World coordinate (WCS) keywords are in the header; Egami does no sky coordinate math.

FitsFormat

FITS (Flexible Image Transport System, standard 4.0), astronomy's image format: headers of 80-character keyword cards in 2880-byte blocks, then big-endian 8, 16, 32 or 64-bit integer or 32 or 64-bit float data. Load gives a viewable picture of the first image, stretched into 0 to 1 (Stretch); LoadAll gives every image of every HDU and every plane of a cube. FitsFile gives the header cards and the physical values themselves. Registered with WithFits(EgamiContext).

FitsHdu

One HDU of a FITS file: its header, and for an image, its BITPIX and axes. A tile-compressed image reports the image's own BITPIX and axes (ZBITPIX, ZNAXISn), not the table's.

FitsHeader

The cards of one FITS header, in order, with typed lookups by keyword. Long string values continued over CONTINUE cards are joined into one card.
