DdsFormat

DDS (DirectDraw Surface), the texture container of Direct3D and most game engines: a header, then every mip level of every face, array slice or depth slice, uncompressed or in BC1 to BC7 blocks. Load gives mip 0 of the first surface; LoadAll and DdsReaderOptions reach the others. Registered with WithDds(EgamiContext).
