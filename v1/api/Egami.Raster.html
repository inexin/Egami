Remarks

A raster is not thread-safe. Several threads may read it at once, but nothing may read while another thread writes. Methods that return a new raster say so in their name (Copy, As); everything else works in place.

Reads the encoded image asynchronously. A stream that cannot seek, such as an upload, is decoded 256 KB at a time as it arrives, so decoding overlaps the wait and only the bytes since the decode's last step are held, not the whole file. Decoding itself is synchronous, since it is CPU-bound.

Transcode(string, string, Action<RasterOps>?, ReaderOptions?, WriterOptions?, EgamiContext?)

Decodes a file, runs a short chain of operations and encodes the result, a band of rows at a time, so a huge image never has to exist whole: a thumbnail of a 20,000 × 20,000 PNG takes a few MB. Remarks

The chain may hold Crop, Resize, Fit and Cover with any filter, FlipHorizontal (and AutoOrient of a mirrored image), and the per-pixel color adjustments (Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, HueRotate, Sepia, Grayscale, Invert, Opacity, ColorMatrix).
