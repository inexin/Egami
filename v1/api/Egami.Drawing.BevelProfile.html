Enum BevelProfile

Namespace
Egami.Drawing
Assembly
Egami.Drawing.dll

The cross-section of a bevel: how its height rises from the shape's edge to the flat face inside it. The names are DrawingML's bevel presets (a:bevelT prst); each is a smooth curve of Egami's own, tuned to the look of its name rather than to another renderer's pixels.

public enum BevelProfile

Fields

Angle = 4

A straight chamfer.

ArtDeco = 11

Three steps, each a short riser and a tread.

Circle = 0

A quarter circle: steep at the edge, level at the face. DrawingML's default.

Convex = 6

A bulge: rises at a finite slope and levels off at the face.

CoolSlant = 3

A steep rise near the edge, then a long shallow slant up to the face.

Cross = 2

A ridge: up to full height halfway in, then down to half height at the face.

Divot = 8

A groove: a raised rim at the edge, a dip, then the face.

HardEdge = 10

A short steep wall, then the face: the bevel takes the first quarter of its width.

RelaxedInset = 1

A gentle S-curve, level at both ends.

Riblet = 9

A slope with three small ribs on it.

Slope = 7

A concave ramp: shallow at the edge, steepening toward the face.

SoftRound = 5

A rounded rise, a quarter sine: firm at the edge, level at the face.

Examples

// A raised button: fill the shape, then draw a bevel over it, lit from the top left.
using Raster<Rgba8> button = Raster.Create(220, 90, Rgba8.White);
using (RasterCanvas<Rgba8> canvas = button.CreateCanvas())
{
    Path shape = Path.RoundRect(new RoundRect(new RectF(10, 10, 200, 70), 16));
    canvas.FillPath(shape, new Rgba8(40, 110, 200, 255));
    canvas.DrawBevel(shape, new Bevel
    {
        Profile = BevelProfile.Circle,
        Width = 10,
        Height = 8,
        Material = BevelMaterial.Plastic,
        Light = BevelLight.FromDirection(LightDirection.TopLeft),
    });
}
