Class WavFormat

Namespace
Egami.Video.Containers
Assembly

WAV (.wav): RIFF WAVE with integer or float PCM, WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE (channel mask and valid bits) and the compressed formats AVI also names, plus RF64 and BW64 for files over 4 GB (Instance) and Sony Wave64 (.w64, Wave64). Streamed files whose sizes were never filled in (0 or 0xFFFFFFFF) are read to the end. LIST/INFO and id3 tags become Tags, and cue points with their labels become chapters. The writer stores PCM and float, INFO tags and chapters as cue points, and turns a WAV file into RF64 by itself when the data passes 4 GB, as EBU Tech 3306 says (a JUNK chunk reserved after the header becomes the ds64 chunk). Seeking is sample exact.

Examples

// WAV, Wave64 and FLAC are containers like the others: remux PCM from WAV into Wave64 without decoding.
MediaFile.Remux("tone.wav", "tone.w64");
MediaInfo info = MediaFile.Probe("tone.w64");
AudioTrackInfo track = (AudioTrackInfo)info.Tracks[0];

Methods

CreateMuxer(Stream, IReadOnlyList<TrackInfo>, MediaMetadata, MediaContext, bool)

Creates a muxer for one audio track of integer PCM (8, 16, 24 or 32 bits, little-endian) or float PCM (32 or 64 bits). Tags go into a LIST/INFO chunk and chapters into a cue chunk with labels; attachments are left out with a "metadata-dropped" warning. A non-seekable stream gets the streaming sizes 0xFFFFFFFF, which readers take as "to the end".
