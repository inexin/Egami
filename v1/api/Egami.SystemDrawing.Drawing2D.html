A line end drawn from a path: the path's x axis runs across the line and its y axis along it, outward from the end, in pen widths. The stroke path is outlined; GDI+ refuses fill paths. Set it as a pen's CustomStartCap or CustomEndCap.

HatchBrush

A brush of one of GDI+'s 53 hatch patterns: 8 by 8 tiles of a foreground color on a background, from the device origin.

LinearGradientBrush

A brush of colors along a line: across a rectangle at an angle (or between two points), the colors constant on lines perpendicular to it, with a Blend or preset colors, repeating beyond the rectangle.

Matrix

A 3 by 2 affine transform, as System.Drawing's Matrix: row vectors times the matrix, so Prepend (the default) applies a new transform before the existing one.

PathData

PathGradientBrush

A brush of colors from a center out to the edge of a polygon or path: the center color at the center point, the surrounding colors at the boundary's points, with a Blend or preset colors between.

RegionData

A region in the EMF+ region format ([MS-EMFPLUS] 2.2.1.8), after GDI+'s size and checksum, from GetRegionData().
