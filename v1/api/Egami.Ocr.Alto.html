Reads and writes ALTO (Analyzed Layout and Text Object), the XML format of library digitization that ABBYY, Tesseract and Transkribus write: pages (Page), lines (TextLine) and words (String) with their positions, WC confidence and LANG.

Examples

// ALTO from ABBYY, Tesseract or a library's digitization: positions in 1/10 mm or 1/1200 inch become pixels at Dpi.
IReadOnlyList<OcrResult> pages = Alto.Parse(File.ReadAllText("page.xml"), new OcrReadOptions { Dpi = 300 });
Console.WriteLine($"{pages[0].ImageSize}: {pages[0].Text}");

// Turn it into hOCR, or write ALTO 4 in pixels.
using FileStream hocr = File.Create("page.hocr");
Hocr.Write(hocr, pages);
using FileStream alto = File.Create("page-v4.xml");
Alto.Write(alto, pages);

Remarks

The reader takes ALTO 1 to 4 by element names, in any namespace. Positions in 1/10 mm or 1/1200 inch become pixels at Dpi. A HYP hyphen joins the word before it. No DTD or schema is ever fetched. The writer writes ALTO 4 in pixels.
