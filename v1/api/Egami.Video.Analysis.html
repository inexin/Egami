Namespace Egami.Video.Analysis

Classes

AudioLevelMeter

Measures sample levels per channel and over all channels: peak, RMS, DC offset, crest factor, clipped samples, how often the peak was reached, the noise floor, zero crossings and a histogram in 1 dB steps. It reports what ffmpeg's volumedetect and astats report, from any sample format.

AudioLevelReport

What AudioLevelMeter measured.

AudioPictures

Draws pictures of audio: a waveform (the min and max of each column with the RMS level shaded inside, as editors and podcast players show it) and a spectrogram (frequency over time, loudness as color). Both read the audio once with constant memory, so a three-hour file costs no more memory than a short one, and return a plain Raster<TPixel> without text; the CLI adds axes and legends.

BlackDetector

Finds black stretches: runs of frames where nearly every luma sample is near black, lasting at least MinDuration. It keeps ffmpeg's blackdetect measure and defaults, with the pixel threshold a fraction of the stream's own range so 8-bit and 10-bit files behave alike.

BlackOptions

What BlackDetector counts as black. The defaults are ffmpeg blackdetect's.

ChannelLevels

The sample levels of one channel, or of all channels together. Levels are in dBFS, where a full-scale sample is 0.

CropDetector

Finds black borders: the rectangle inside letterbox, pillarbox or windowbox bars, as ffmpeg's cropdetect does in its black mode. Rows and columns whose mean luma is at or under Limit count as border. Each frame's rectangle is rounded to Round; Report() gives the rectangle that keeps every frame's picture, the most common one, and the runs of frames that share one.

CropOptions

What CropDetector counts as a border. The defaults follow ffmpeg cropdetect.

CropReport

What CropDetector found.

CropRun

A run of frames that share one crop rectangle.

DetectedRange

A stretch of time a detector found: black, frozen, duplicated or silent.

DuplicateDetector

Finds repeated frames with ffmpeg mpdecimate's test, without dropping anything: a frame repeats the last kept frame when none of its 8 by 8 luma blocks differs by more than High and no more than Fraction of them differ by more than Low. A block's difference is the mean absolute difference of its samples as a share of the range, so the thresholds mean the same at every bit depth.

DuplicateOptions

What DuplicateDetector counts as a repeat. The defaults are ffmpeg mpdecimate's.

Fade

A fade to or from black (or white, when asked for) that SceneDetector found.

FreezeDetector

Finds frozen stretches: runs of frames that stay within Noise of the frame that starts them, lasting at least MinDuration. The measure is ffmpeg freezedetect's: the mean absolute difference over the Y, Cb and Cr planes as a fraction of the largest code value, against the same -60 dB noise floor.

FreezeOptions

What FreezeDetector counts as frozen. The defaults are ffmpeg freezedetect's.

InterlaceDetector

Tells progressive, interlaced and telecined video apart, as ffmpeg's idet does. Each frame's fields are woven three ways (with each other, and each with the other field of the frame before) and the combing of each weave compared: the weave of fields closest in time combs least. Fields that repeat the frame before's are counted, and repeats in a five-frame pattern mark 3:2 telecine. Motion is needed to decide; frames without it count as undetermined.

InterlaceOptions

How sure InterlaceDetector must be before it decides. The defaults are ffmpeg idet's.

InterlaceReport

What InterlaceDetector counted.

MediaAnalysis

Analyzes a file in one pass: decodes its video and audio once each and feeds every detector the options ask for, then returns what they found. The video and audio are read side by side in time order, so progress moves evenly through the file.

MediaAnalysisOptions

What Run(string, MediaAnalysisOptions?, MediaContext?, IProgress<MediaProgress>?, CancellationToken) looks for and where.

MediaAnalysisResult

What Run(string, MediaAnalysisOptions?, MediaContext?, IProgress<MediaProgress>?, CancellationToken) found.

Scene

A scene: a run of frames from one cut to the next, as SceneDetector finds them.

SceneCut

A point where a new scene starts, as SceneDetector reports it while frames come in.

SceneDetector

Finds the scenes of a video: where one shot cuts to the next, and where a fade or cut through black separates two. Each frame is summarized from its planes (a 16 by 9 grid of mean Y, Cb and Cr and a luma histogram) and scored against the frame before by Method; a high enough score starts a scene, unless it comes within the minimum scene length or turns out to be a flash. Feed it every frame with Add(YuvVideoFrame) and call Complete(MediaTime) at the end, or let Run(string, MediaAnalysisOptions?, MediaContext?, IProgress<MediaProgress>?, CancellationToken) do it. Memory stays flat over any length of video unless KeepSeries is set.

SceneFrames

Reads a picture of each scene, such as for a shot list or a storyboard.

SceneOptions

How SceneDetector finds scenes. The defaults suit most footage; lower the threshold to find softer cuts.

SignalFrame

One frame's measures from SignalMeter.

SignalMeter

Measures the signal, as ffmpeg's signalstats and colordetect do: the lowest, 10th percentile, mean, 90th percentile and highest code values of Y, Cb and Cr; chroma saturation; the share of pixels outside broadcast range (16 to 235 for luma, 16 to 240 for chroma, scaled to the bit depth); which range the samples really use; and whether an alpha plane is used and how. With Blur and Blockiness it also scores sharpness and coding blocks, as blurdetect and blockdetect do.

SignalOptions

What SignalMeter measures besides levels. The blur and blockiness settings follow ffmpeg's blurdetect and blockdetect.

SignalReport

What SignalMeter measured over every frame.

SilenceDetector

Finds silent stretches: runs of samples whose level stays under Noise for at least MinDuration. By default a stretch is silent only when every channel is, as ffmpeg's silencedetect does; PerChannel also finds each channel's own stretches. Times are exact to the sample.

SilenceOptions

What SilenceDetector counts as silence. The defaults are ffmpeg silencedetect's.

SpectrogramOptions

The size, window, scale and colors of an AudioPictures spectrogram.

TestAudioFormat

The rate, channels and frame size of the audio TestSources generates.

TestSources

Generates test video and audio: a moving test pattern with frame numbers and timecode, SMPTE color bars, solid colors and gradients, and sine tones, silence, white or pink noise and sweeps. The frames come out one at a time, so any length costs the same memory; hand them to WriteFrame(VideoFrame) and WriteAudio(AudioFrame), or to a detector. Dispose each frame.

TestVideoFormat

The size, rate and planes of the video TestSources generates.

WaveformOptions

The size, layout and colors of an AudioPictures waveform.

Interfaces

IAudioDetector

A detector that listens to decoded audio one frame at a time. Feed it every frame in time order with Add(AudioFrame), then call Complete(MediaTime) once at the end; Run(string, MediaAnalysisOptions?, MediaContext?, IProgress<MediaProgress>?, CancellationToken) does both from one decode. A detector reads the samples while Add(AudioFrame) runs and keeps no reference to the frame.

IVideoDetector

A detector that looks at decoded pictures one at a time, such as SceneDetector. Feed it every frame in presentation order with Add(YuvVideoFrame), then call Complete(MediaTime) once at the end; Run(string, MediaAnalysisOptions?, MediaContext?, IProgress<MediaProgress>?, CancellationToken) does both for every detector from one decode, and an app with its own decode loop can do the same. A detector reads the frame while Add(YuvVideoFrame) runs and keeps no reference to it, so the caller may dispose the frame right after.
