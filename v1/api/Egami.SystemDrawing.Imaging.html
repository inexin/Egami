Namespace Egami.SystemDrawing.Imaging

Classes

BitmapData

A locked area of a bitmap's pixels, from LockBits(Rectangle, ImageLockMode, PixelFormat): where the first row starts, the bytes from one row to the next, and the size and format of the pixels.

CachedBitmap

A copy of a bitmap ready to draw at device pixels with DrawCachedBitmap(CachedBitmap, int, int).

ColorMap

A color a remap table replaces, and its replacement.

ColorMatrix

A 5 by 5 color matrix: a color's red, green, blue, alpha and 1, as a row from 0 to 1, times the matrix, as GDI+ applies it. Starts as the identity.

ColorPalette

The colors an indexed bitmap's pixels point into. Palette hands out a copy: change its entries and set it back to change the bitmap's palette.

Encoder

An encoder setting, by the GUID GDI+ gives it: quality, compression, color depth, save flags and the rest.

EncoderParameter

One encoder setting and its values, passed to a save in EncoderParameters.

EncoderParameters

The encoder settings of one save.

FrameDimension

How an image's frames are counted: as pages (TIFF), as moments in time (an animated GIF) or as resolutions.

ImageAttributes

How images are recolored when drawn and what is drawn beyond their edges: color matrices, gamma, threshold, a color key, remap tables and an output channel per ColorAdjustType, and a wrap mode. Without a wrap mode set, nothing is drawn beyond an image, as in GDI+.

ImageCodecInfo

One of the codecs Save(Stream, ImageCodecInfo, EncoderParameters?) writes with, as GDI+ lists them: BMP, JPEG, GIF, TIFF and PNG encoders, and those plus EMF, WMF and ICO decoders. Egami reads many more formats than the decoders listed; the list matches GDI+'s so code that looks a codec up by its MIME type or GUID finds the same one.

ImageFormat

An image file format, by the GUID GDI+ gives it: what RawFormat reports and what Save(Stream, ImageFormat) writes.

MetaHeader

A WMF's own header ([MS-WMF] 2.3.2.2).

Metafile

A WMF, EMF or EMF+ picture, read and drawn by Egami.Metafiles: an Image sized by its frame, which DrawImage(Image, float, float) plays as vectors and EnumerateMetafile(Metafile, PointF, EnumerateMetafileProc) walks record by record. Egami reads metafiles but does not record them, so the recording constructors throw.

MetafileHeader

A metafile's header: its type, size, resolution and version, as GDI+ reports them.

PropertyItem

One metadata tag of an image, as EXIF and TIFF store it: its id, type and raw bytes. PropertyItems and GetPropertyItem(int) read them from the image's EXIF data, and SetPropertyItem(PropertyItem) writes one back. There is no public constructor, as in System.Drawing: change an item read from an image.

WmfPlaceableFileHeader

A placeable WMF's header: its box in its own units and how many make an inch.
