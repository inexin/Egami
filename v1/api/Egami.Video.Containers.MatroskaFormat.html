Matroska (RFC 9559) and its WebM subset. Both instances read either; they differ in the DocType and extension a muxer writes: Instance writes Matroska (.mkv), WebM writes WebM (.webm).

Methods

CreateMuxer(Stream, IReadOnlyList<TrackInfo>, MediaMetadata, MediaContext, bool)

Creates a muxer. Tracks are numbered from 1 in the order given, timestamps are stored in milliseconds, and a Cluster starts on a video keyframe once about a second or 5 MB has passed. On a non-seekable stream the Segment has an unknown size and there are no Cues. WebM holds only VP8, VP9, AV1, Opus, Vorbis and WebVTT.
