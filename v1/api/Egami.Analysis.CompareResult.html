Examples

// Every cheap metric in one pass, per channel and overall, on the 0 to 1 scale; SSIM on request.
CompareResult result = Raster.Compare(expected, actual, new CompareOptions { Ssim = true, Fuzz = 0.02f });
Console.WriteLine($"PSNR {result.Overall.Psnr:F1} dB, SSIM {result.Overall.Ssim:F4}, {result.DifferentPixels} pixels off by more than 2%");
bool sameLook = result.Overall.Psnr >= 40;

Butteraugli

Gets the Butteraugli distance (libjxl's psychovisual metric, as butteraugli_main prints it): the largest difference the model of the eye finds anywhere, where under about 1 the images look the same. NaN unless Butteraugli was set.

Remarks

The color channels are read as sRGB, or as linear light when the image's profile has a linear curve, with sRGB primaries, 1.0 being 80 nits. Images with alpha are compared over black and over white, and the larger distance counts.

Butteraugli3Norm

Gets the Butteraugli 3-norm: the mean of the distance map's 3-, 6- and 12-norms, which follows the overall quality more than the single worst spot does. NaN unless Butteraugli was set.
