An Australia Post 4-State barcode's format, by its Format Control Code (FCC).

public enum AustraliaPostFormat

Fields

Customer2 = 59

Customer Barcode 2 (FCC 59): the DPID and 16 bars of customer information, 52 bars.

Customer3 = 62

Customer Barcode 3 (FCC 62): the DPID and 31 bars of customer information, 67 bars.

Redirection = 92

Australia Post's redirection barcode (FCC 92), for its internal use: the DPID alone, 37 bars.

ReplyPaid = 45

Australia Post's Reply Paid barcode (FCC 45), for its internal use: the DPID alone, 37 bars.

Routing = 87

Australia Post's routing barcode (FCC 87), for its internal use: the DPID alone, 37 bars.

Standard = 11

The Standard Customer Barcode (FCC 11): the DPID alone, 37 bars.

Examples

// A Standard Customer Barcode holds the eight-digit DPID; customer information after it takes Customer Barcode 2 or 3.
Barcode mail = Barcode.AustraliaPost("39549554");
Barcode withInformation = Barcode.AustraliaPost("54516251ABC123");
Barcode digits = Barcode.AustraliaPost("5451625112345", new AustraliaPostOptions { Format = AustraliaPostFormat.Customer3 });

// Drawn at a 0.55 mm module (here in millimetres): 37 bars, 40.15 mm long and 4.4 mm tall.
Path bars = mail.ToPath(moduleSize: 0.55f);
Console.WriteLine(mail.HumanReadableText); // 11 39549554 54 17 53 58, the FCC, the DPID and the check symbols
