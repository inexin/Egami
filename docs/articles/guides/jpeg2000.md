# JPEG 2000

Egami reads JPEG 2000 (ITU-T T.800 | ISO/IEC 15444-1, all of Part 1), written from the 2019 edition of T.800, T.801's JPX boxes
and the PDF specification's JPXDecode filter. `Jpeg2000Format.Info` is registered in `EgamiContext.Default`; JPEG 2000 is never
written. Every ISO/IEC 15444-4 conformance codestream decodes within its tolerances: the reversible ones to OpenJPEG 2.5.4's
samples exactly, the irreversible ones within one level, at full resolution and with levels dropped.

## Reading files

JP2 and JPX files start with the JP2 signature box and raw codestreams (J2K, J2C, a PDF's JPXDecode stream) with SOC and SIZ, so
`Raster.Load` recognizes both. `MinimumSize` drops whole wavelet levels, JPEG 2000's own reduction (a quarter of the levels'
work for each level dropped), and `Region` decodes only the tiles and code-blocks the rectangle needs, so a thumbnail of a large
scan or a viewport into a map costs a fraction of the whole decode. Rows stream a row of tiles at a time.

## What it loads as

`Jpeg2000ReaderOptions.Components` chooses between two readings of the same samples.

| `Jpeg2000Components` | Pixels |
| --- | --- |
| `Color` (default) | Palettes applied, channels placed by the channel definitions, sYCC and CIELab converted to sRGB; gray, RGB or CMYK, with alpha when a channel is opacity |
| `Raw` | The codestream's components after its own component transform: 1 as gray, 2 as gray and alpha, 3 as RGB, 4 as CMYK, for a PDF that names its own color space |

Depths widen to the next Egami type by bit replication, so the stored value is recoverable by a shift: 1 to 8 bits load as
`Gray8`, `Rgb8`, `Rgba8` or `Cmyk8`, 9 to 16 bits as their 16-bit forms, deeper ones as float types. Signed samples are offset
by half their range, with the `Jpeg2000.SignedSamples` warning. Premultiplied alpha loads straight. ICC profiles go to
`RasterMetadata.IccProfile` and the pixels stay as stored, as for every format; XMP, the resolution box and COM markers are read.

Subsampled sYCC (4:2:0 and 4:2:2) is upsampled with the YUV kernels at JPEG 2000's chroma siting, or loads straight into a
`YuvRaster` without upsampling; `FastUpsampling` repeats chroma as OpenJPEG does instead. `MaxQualityLayers` decodes only the
first layers of a layered file, for a quick preview.

## What is decoded

| Part | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Codestream | SIZ, COD, COC, QCD, QCC, RGN, POC, PPM, PPT and COM; TLM, PLM, PLT and CRG read past; SOP and EPH; tiles and tile-parts in any order |
| Tier 2 | Packet headers with tag trees, all five progression orders, progression order changes, precincts of any size, packed packet headers |
| Tier 1 | The three coding passes and every code-block style: arithmetic bypass, context reset, termination on each pass, vertically causal contexts, predictable termination, segmentation symbols; ROI by max-shift |
| Reconstruction | Reversible 5/3 and irreversible 9/7 wavelets at any number of levels, odd sizes and offsets; RCT and ICT; subsampled and signed components |
| JP2 and JPX | ihdr, colr (enumerated, restricted and any ICC), pclr, cmap, cdef, res, XMP uuid; JPX's several colr boxes and rreq; the first codestream |
| Refused | Part 2 coding tools and High-Throughput (Part 15) code-blocks throw `NotSupportedException` naming the feature |

`Jpeg2000Details` describes what was decoded: the `Jpeg2000Container`, the profile, each `Jpeg2000Component`'s precision, sign
and sampling, the wavelet, levels, layers and `Jpeg2000ProgressionOrder`, the tiling, the `Jpeg2000ColorSpace`, whether there is
a palette, the `Jpeg2000Channel` definitions with their `Jpeg2000ChannelType`, and the comments.

## Speed and threads

Code-blocks are independent, so tier 1 runs across cores by `EgamiContext.MaxParallelism`, with the same output at any thread
count; the wavelets and color conversions are vectorized. On a 10-megapixel photo Egami decodes lossy files faster than OpenJPEG on one
thread and every file two to four times faster on all cores.

## Damaged and hostile data

Every size in SIZ is checked against `SafetyLimits` before a tile is read; the number of tiles, components, levels, passes and
packet lengths are bounded by the standard and by the data; the samples held count against `MaxTotalBytes`, and a codestream
whose tiles and components would build far more structure than its samples fill is refused. A codestream that ends early decodes
what it holds with the `Jpeg2000.Truncated` warning, and throws `CorruptDataException` in strict mode.
