Description:
  Trace images into vector shapes (logos, line art, scans, posters, photos, pixel art) and write them as SVG.

Usage:
  egami trace <inputs>... [options]

Arguments:
  <inputs>  Image files, globs (*, ?, **) expanded the same way in every shell, folders with --recursive, or - for standard input.

Options:
  -o, --output <path>                  Where to write the SVG: a file, a folder, a template with {name}, {ext}, {dir} and {index}, or - (the default for one input) for standard output.
  -p, --preset <preset>                The starting settings: default, black-and-white, logo, poster, photo, line-art, sketch, pixel-art. default (black and white with a white background) by default; the options below change it.
  -m, --mode <mode>                    How pixels become shapes: black-and-white, grayscale, color, logo, centerline (strokes along the middle of lines) or pixel-art (smooth shapes from sprites and other pixel art).
  -c, --colors <count>                 The most colors (gray levels in grayscale) to reduce the image to, 2 to 256 (grayscale, color, logo).
  --palette <colors>                   Fixed colors instead, CSS colors separated by commas or spaces, such as "black, #c00, white" (grayscale, color, logo).
  --layering <stacked|abutting>        stacked (each color layer covers those above it, the default) or abutting (each color only its own area).
  -t, --threshold <0-1>                The level that splits ink from paper, 0 (black) to 1 (white); found by Otsu's method by default (black-and-white, centerline).
  --path-fidelity <0-1>                How closely curves follow the pixels, 0 (fewest curves) to 1 (tightest).
  --corners <0-1>                      How readily corners stay sharp, 0 (round every corner) to 1 (a polygon).
  --noise <pixels>                     The smallest region kept, in pixels; smaller ones merge into those around them.
  --ignore-white                       Leave out white: no white background shape, and no white color layer.
  --smoothing <0-1>                    Edge-keeping smoothing before the colors are found, 0 to 1, for photos (grayscale, color).
  --snap-lines                         Make straight runs lines (grayscale, color, logo; on by default).
  --snap-arcs                          Make runs on a circle arcs, and round outlines circles (grayscale, color, logo; on by default).
  --stroke-width <pixels>              One pen width in pixels for every stroke, instead of the width each stroke's ink covers (centerline).
  --exact                              Keep the pixel squares, one shape per color with straight edges, instead of smoothing them (pixel-art).
  --precision <digits>                 Decimal places of the SVG's coordinates.
  --preview-png <path>                 Also write the trace drawn as a PNG, the image's size: a file, a folder or a template with {name}.
  -r, --recursive                      Take every image in folders given as inputs, at any depth.
  -j, --jobs <count>                   How many files to work on at once; 0 (the default) for one per processor.
  --in-place                           Allow outputs to replace their inputs.
  --fail-fast                          Stop at the first file that fails.
  -?, -h, --help                       Show help and usage information
  -v, --verbose                        Write information to standard error.
  -vv, --debug                         Write debugging detail to standard error.
  -q, --quiet                          Write only errors to standard error.
  --limit <name=value,...>             Safety limits, such as pixels=100M,memory=2GB,frames=500 (names: pixels, width, height, frames, memory, metadata). EGAMI_LIMITS sets them too.
  --preview-formats                    Also read and write the formats in preview (DICOM in this release); they may change or go in any minor release.
  --raw-output <value>                 What a camera RAW input loads as: developed (default), half-size, preview or mosaic.
  --raw-preview <value>                Which embedded preview --raw-output preview loads, 0 for the largest.
  --raw-white-balance <value>          White balance of a RAW develop: as-shot (default), auto or custom (with --raw-temperature and --raw-tint).
  --raw-temperature <value>            Color temperature in kelvin for --raw-white-balance custom.
  --raw-tint <value>                   Tint for --raw-white-balance custom, positive toward magenta.
  --raw-exposure <value>               Exposure of a RAW develop in stops, such as +0.5 or -1.
  --raw-demosaic <value>               Demosaic method: ahd (default), malvar or bilinear.
  --raw-highlights <value>             Clipped highlights: clip (default), blend or rebuild.
  --raw-noise-reduction <value>        Luminance noise reduction of a RAW develop, 0 to 100 (default 0).
  --raw-color-noise-reduction <value>  Color noise reduction of a RAW develop, 0 to 100 (default 25).
  --raw-lens-profiles <value>          Folders of Lensfun (.xml) and Adobe (.lcp) lens profiles, separated by the system's path separator.
  --raw-no-lens-corrections            Apply no lens corrections.
  --raw-auto-ca                        Measure and correct lateral chromatic aberration when no lens profile covers the file.
  --raw-tone-curve                     Give a develop without a profile tone curve the default contrast curve.
  --raw-no-profile                     Leave out the camera profile's look (HueSatMap, LookTable and tone curve).
  --animate <fps>                      Load an animated SVG input as all its frames, this many a second (30 when no rate is given): one loop, or up to the end of its last animation, at most 10 seconds.
  --at <time>                          Draw an SVG input as it looks this long after it loads, such as 1.5s, 1500ms or 1.5 (seconds); with --animate, the first frame's moment.
